# GIAS-AIS-001: AI System Assurance Standard

## Purpose

This standard describes assurance criteria and evaluation methods for AI systems used in regulated contexts.

## Scope

Applies to AI systems that materially affect individual rights or safety.

## Sections

### Risk Management

Approach to identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks.

### Transparency

Requirements for reporting and documentation.

### Testing & Assurance

Guidance on testing regimes and evidence collection.
